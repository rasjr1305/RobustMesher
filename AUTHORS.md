# Authorship

RobustMesher repository maintained by Romildo Junior.

The meshing, geometry, sizing, and Winslow routines originate from [Spyro](https://github.com/NDF-Poli-USP/spyro) and [PR #359](https://github.com/NDF-Poli-USP/spyro/pull/359), with standalone adapters and native 3D smoothing. Original Spyro authorship and the LGPLv3-or-later license are retained.

The layout and documentation organization follow [PakMsh](https://github.com/NDF-Poli-USP/PakMsh).
